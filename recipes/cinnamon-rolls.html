Apple Butter Cinnamon Rolls: Soft & Gooey Apple Butter Cinnamon Rolls

Ingrediants:

    For the dough
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2¼ tsp instant yeast
  • ¼ cup granulated sugar
  • ½ tsp salt
  • ¾ cup warm milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp melted butter
    • For the filling
  • ¾ cup apple butter
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 tbsp softened butter
    • For the glaze
  • 1 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp milk
  • ½ tsp vanilla extract

Steps:

  1. Combine flour, yeast, sugar, and salt in a large bowl.
  2. Add warm milk, egg, and melted butter. Mix until a soft dough forms.
  3. Knead for 5–7 minutes, then cover and let rise for about 1 hour, or until doubled in size.
  4. Roll the dough into a large rectangle.
  5. Spread softened butter over the dough, followed by apple butter.
  6. Sprinkle evenly with brown sugar and cinnamon.
  7. Roll the dough tightly from the long side into a log.
  8. Cut into 10–12 rolls and place in a greased baking dish.
  9. Cover and let rise for another 30 minutes.
  10. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 22–27 minutes, until golden brown.
  11. Mix powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla to make the glaze.
  12. Drizzle the glaze over the warm cinnamon rolls and serve.
