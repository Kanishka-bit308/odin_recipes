Buffalo Chicken and Roasted Potato

Buffalo Chicken Roasted Potato: Crispy, Spicy & Cheesy Buffalo Chicken Potatoes

Ingrediants:

  • 4 medium potatoes, cubed
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • ½ cup buffalo sauce
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • ½ tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp ranch or blue cheese dressing
  • 2 tbsp chopped green onions
  • 1 tbsp chopped parsley

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
  2. Toss cubed potatoes with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread potatoes on a baking sheet and roast for 25–30 minutes, turning halfway through, until golden and crispy.
  4. Mix shredded chicken with buffalo sauce.
  5. Remove potatoes from the oven and top with buffalo chicken and shredded cheddar.
  6. Return to the oven for 5–7 minutes, until the cheese melts.
  7. Drizzle with ranch or blue cheese dressing.
  8. Garnish with green onions and parsley before serving.
